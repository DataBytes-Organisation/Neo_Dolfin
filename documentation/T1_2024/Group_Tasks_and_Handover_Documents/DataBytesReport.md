DataBytes is a multi-disciplinary company that researches data-driven
solutions and creates platforms and applications. We work with
cutting-edge technology in the fields of artificial intelligence, data
science and digital finance to develop solutions that will have a
positive global impact.

Our teams cover a variety of sectors including environmental

leverage AI processing technologies.

Databytes current projects include:

1.  **DolFin** - An innovative financial wellness platform that
    integrates open banking data and utilizes advanced Artificial
    Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) technologies to optimize
    financial management.

    spot endangered species in the Otways, underlining our dedication to
    environmental preservation.

    safeguard personal information while facilitating secure data
    sharing for intelligence purposes.

**Overview, Goals, and Objectives**

DolFin is an innovative financial well-being platform that integrates
open banking data and leverages cutting-edge artificial intelligence
(AI) and machine learning (ML) technologies to streamline financial
management, offer personalized forecasts and advice, and deliver these
insights through a smart chatbot interface.

At the heart of the DolFin project is the ambition to overcome the
limitations of the traditional banking system, characterized by
monopolistic behaviours and unequal service provision to different
demographics. By offering an open, accessible platform, DolFin aims to
enable effective financial management for everyone, regardless of their
economic status.

The long-term goals of the DolFin project extend beyond immediate
financial management aid. It aims to foster financial literacy,
encourage healthy financial habits, and ultimately contribute to
reducing the economic inequality gap. By empowering users with knowledge
and tools to make informed financial decisions, DolFin intends to create
a more inclusive financial ecosystem that benefits everyone, not just
those who can afford traditional advisory services.

The stakeholders of the DolFin project include Deakin Capstone students,
who are integral to its development and implementation; the company
director, who oversees the strategic direction of the project; Deakin
staff, who provide academic support and guidance; and, most importantly,
individuals seeking to improve their financial well-being. For these
stakeholders, DolFin promises not only to revolutionize the way they
interact with their finances but also to provide them with a platform
that supports their financial growth and resilience.

financial crime by using advanced privacy technologies such as
Homomorphic Encryption, Differential Privacy, Secure Multi-party
Computation, and Federated Learning.

facilitating secure data sharing for intelligence purposes. The project
strikes a balance between the rigorous demands of financial intelligence
and the legal and ethical imperatives of privacy protection.

The long-term goals of PTFI are to produce a public online resource with
privacy focussed research which will advance privacy technology, to
conduct experiments and implement privacy technologies with financial
intelligence use cases, and to develop viable products which serve
financial privacy use cases.

in its development, the company director and board, privacy researchers
and developers, and the public. Our research is intended for public
consumption and most of our work will be made available via GitHub as
the project progresses. This open access principle will assist others
involved in privacy technology to benefit from our experiments, mistakes
and research outputs.

**Project DiscountMate**

**Overview, Goals and Objectives:**

Imagine a world where you can effortlessly discover the best deals on
your favourite items across various supermarket chains, potentially
saving hundreds of dollars every year. DiscountMate aims to simplify the
grocery shopping experience for consumers by providing them with
accurate and timely information about discounted items available at
various supermarket chains.

Through the platform, users can save both time and money by easily
identifying the best deals on their favourite products. By leveraging
data collection techniques such as web scraping, DiscountMate ensures
that users have access to up-to-date pricing information from multiple
providers, allowing them to make informed purchasing decisions.

In addition to offering real-time price comparisons, DiscountMate
utilizes machine learning and data analysis techniques to predict future
discount opportunities and provide personalized recommendations based on
user preferences and purchase history. By analysing user interactions,
such as item searches and purchase receipts, the platform continuously
learns and adapts to better serve its users.

**Enhance User Experience:** Develop an intuitive and user-friendly
interface that allows users to effortlessly browse through ongoing deals
and discounts.

**Aggregate Discounts:** Collate discounts and offers from multiple
supermarkets into a centralized platform for easy access and comparison.

**Personalized Recommendations:** Implement algorithms to analyse user
preferences and shopping patterns, providing personalized
recommendations tailored to individual needs.

**Seamless Integration:** Integrate with supermarket loyalty programs
and rewards systems to enhance user engagement and incentivize repeat
purchases.

**Data Security:** Implement robust security measures to safeguard user
data and ensure compliance with data protection regulations.

**Aims for Trimester:**

**Platform Development:** Build and deploy the DiscountMate platform,
incorporating features such as real-time price comparisons, personalized
recommendations, and user-friendly interface design.

**Data Integration:** Integrate data sources to ensure seamless
retrieval and processing of grocery data from various supermarket
websites. Implement robust data management practices to maintain data
accuracy and consistency.

**User Engagement:** Foster user engagement and retention through
targeted marketing efforts, user incentives, and community-building
initiatives. Continuously gather user feedback to improve platform
functionality and user experience.

**Scalability and Reliability:** Develop a scalable and reliable
infrastructure to accommodate growing user demand and ensure
uninterrupted service delivery. Implement monitoring and maintenance
procedures to address any potential issues proactively.

**Trimester Deliverables:**

**Market Research:** Conduct in-depth research on five competitors to
gain insights into their offerings, market positioning, and user
demographics. Compile findings into a comprehensive market research
document to inform decision-making.

**Solution Document (SRS):** Develop a Software Requirements
Specifications Document outlining the overall solution, key features,
user stories, epics, and customer personas. This document serves as a
blueprint for the project\'s development and guides the implementation
process.

**Figma Designs:** Create mock website designs for DiscountMate to
visualize the user interface and determine the optimal design layout.
Finalize the design based on feedback and consensus within the team.

**Data Science - EDA on Data:** Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
on grocery data to gain insights into pricing trends, product
popularity, and user preferences. Use the findings to refine the
platform\'s recommendation algorithms and enhance user experience.

**Web Scraper Testing:** Test the web scraper developed in the previous
trimester on current grocery websites to ensure its functionality and
reliability. Make necessary adjustments and improvements to optimize
data collection processes.

**Long-term Deliverables:**

**Fully Developed Platform:** A fully developed and operational
DiscountMate platform accessible to users across multiple devices and
platforms, offering comprehensive features and functionality.
User-friendly interface for searching and browsing grocery items.

**Enhanced Data Analysis:** Advanced algorithms for analysing user data
and predicting future discount opportunities, providing users with
Personalized deal recommendations based on user preferences and purchase
history.

**Continuous Improvement:** Ongoing updates and enhancements to the
platform based on user feedback and market trends, ensuring that
DiscountMate remains relevant and competitive in the long run.

**User Community:** A thriving user community built around DiscountMate,
fostering engagement, loyalty, and advocacy among users through targeted
marketing initiatives and community-building efforts.

**Partnerships and Collaborations:** Strategic partnerships and
collaborations with supermarket chains, brands, and other stakeholders
to expand the platform\'s reach, enhance its offerings, and create
mutually beneficial opportunities for growth.

**Project Members:**

For the current trimester, our project team consists solely of junior
members. The project encompasses three key areas: UI Design, Market
Research, and Data Science. Members are free to choose and be associated
with multiple areas based on their interests and core competencies. A
weekly stand-up meeting is held to keep members updated on tasks and
progress during the sprints. Priority tasks are identified in each
sprint; however, members are able to propose new tasks that they deem
necessary for achieving the project\'s goals.
